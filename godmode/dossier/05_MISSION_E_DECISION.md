# Mission E — Decision memo: the first venture

**Decision.** Launch **LabelLedger**. Its job: *keep every generic label in sync with its reference, and prove it.*

**Status.** Proposed by the virtual founding team. It needs the founder's approval to start outreach.

## Positioning

> **For** labeling and regulatory-affairs teams at generic-medicine makers and the consultancies that serve them,
> **who** must keep their labels aligned with the reference label and document every assessment,
> **LabelLedger** is a version-controlled label ledger
> **that** shows exactly what changed, how much it matters, and where your label has drifted, with sources attached.
> **Unlike** enterprise label-intelligence suites, it is self-serve, transparently priced, generics-first, and built on deterministic redlines that AI only explains and never invents.

## Why it beats the alternatives

| Versus | Why LabelLedger first |
|---|---|
| O02 RIM-lite for exporters | O01 can show value **before** a customer shares any data (public labels), which shortens every sales cycle. Buyers have higher budgets (US-market obligations). The engine O01 needs (parser, archive, alerts) is the foundation O02 reuses. O02 is next, not never. |
| O05 Query copilot / O04 Dossier copilot | Both require confidential dossiers from day one, from a vendor nobody has heard of. O01 earns the trust they need. |
| O03 Requirements intelligence | Content treadmill and unproven willingness to pay. It works better as the free SEO layer feeding O02. |
| O50 Productized service | Not a separate business. It is how we sell O01 during validation: concierge drift reports while the self-serve product matures. |
| O25 Exam prep / O26 Cosmetics checker | Faster cash but weak moats, small ceilings, and none of the founder's regulatory edge. |
| O18 npcadd Cloud | Strong founder fit, weak budgets. It stays open source as a credibility asset. |

## What we are *not* doing (yet)

- No EU or emerging-market label sources until US generics validate.
- No dossier uploads, no validated-GxP claims, no SOC 2 audit until customers require it.
- No fundraising. The base case needs about $1.1k of external cash (`../finance/results/model.md`).
- No hires before about $5k MRR, except a part-time regulatory specialist in the base plan from month 15.
- No company registration or spending without the founder's explicit approval.

## Conditions that would reopen this decision

- Kill criteria in `04_MISSION_D_RED_TEAM.md` are met. Pivot to O02.
- An incumbent launches a self-serve generics tier at or below our price within 90 days. Re-position toward emerging-market labels plus RIM-lite.
- FDA finalises the Patient Medication Information rule **[E15]**. Accelerate: every Rx label would need new structured patient content, which is a demand spike for label tooling.

## Success at 12 months (base case, assumption-based)

- About 16 paying teams and about $6.6k MRR, an annualised run-rate of about $79k.
- Monthly break-even including founder draw around month 6 (`../finance/results/model.md`).
- About 1,000 weekly-digest subscribers.
- A label archive covering every watched reference label.
- An evaluation set that supports a published accuracy statement.

These are scenarios, not promises. The conservative case is about 5 teams and $1.4k MRR at month 12.
