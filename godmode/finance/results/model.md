## What would break this model

1. **Close rate.** Every scenario assumes buyers convert after a demo. If the first 30 demos
   convert below 10%, the base case moves to the conservative case — re-price or re-position.
2. **Churn.** At 5%/month, lifetime falls to 20 months and LTV roughly halves versus base.
3. **Founder draw.** It is the largest cost line in year 1. It is what the runway buys;
   lowering it extends runway but not the business case.
4. **Payment rails.** Selling from Bangladesh depends on a merchant of record or a foreign
   entity; fees and payout friction are modelled at 5% + $0.50 and must be verified.
5. **Services dependency.** Early revenue includes concierge packages that consume founder
   time; if they do not convert into subscriptions, they are a consultancy, not a product.
